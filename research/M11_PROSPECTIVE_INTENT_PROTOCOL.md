# M11 — Prospective source-disjoint task-intent study protocol

**Project status:** research engineering only. The M8 challenge and M9 prompts were developed after observing previous failures. This prevents claims of *independent* validation based on the 30 already-seen queries. M10 owner-run local analysis reported at threshold 0: **2/10 true symptom-information queries retained**, **8/10 symptom queries falsely abstained** and **7/20 nonclinical queries incorrectly accepted**. Margin range approximately -0.0814 to +0.0744. This is a post-hoc finding to motivate a prospective study, not clinical validation.

## Hypothesis and task definitions — freeze before gathering/scoring new annotations

**Research question:** Can a separately labeled, independently authored query-purpose cohort distinguish a query about *experienced symptoms* from a nonclinical task that happens to mention medical terms, while preserving high positive-symptom acceptance? We must not use performance against the M8 examples for threshold selection or claims of generalization.

Five mutually exclusive labels:
- `symptom_information`: a person is describing symptoms (real or fictional for research purposes) or explicitly asking for condition information relevant to those symptoms. Not an approved diagnosis.
- `nonclinical_task`: an unrelated task such as coding, writing, academic citation, finance, media or creative text; may contain disease names in a nonclinical context.
- `general_health_information`: medical education without a report of experienced symptoms. Neither clinical negative nor nonclinical negative. Report separately.
- `clinical_out_of_catalog`: symptom-oriented query with a condition intentionally absent from MediPower's 29-condition catalog. Its *intent* is positive; it is a separate **open-set retrieval** risk, not a nonclinical negative.
- `ambiguous_or_mixed`: multiple intentions or insufficient information. Record and analyze separately; do not force a binary label.

A nonclinical negative is NOT a clinical out-of-catalog case. For a binary exploratory intent gate, only symptom_information vs nonclinical_task are included; the other three classes must be reported as separate exclusions, then examined in a distinct protocol. This scheme is a design decision, not a clinical taxonomy.

## Data sourcing and independent annotation

1. **Recruit a source/author set independently of the existing M8/M9 scripts and development examples.** Do not feed annotators the 30 M8 items, past failures or M9 prototype texts. The author, instruction/template family and source should be documented. LLM-authored cases generated by these project scripts or the same person reviewing the failures do NOT count as a blinded external test cohort.
2. **Secure rights and privacy:** use fictional, consented synthetic scenarios or properly licensed public text; store evidence of rights/attribution and a non-sensitive `source_origin`. Do not ask real patients to share identifying records through this research prototype. A CSV field claiming permission is only an assertion and cannot verify legal rights.
3. **Two independent human annotators**, blinded to model predictions and each other's labels, label query intent. Distinct annotator IDs are checked programmatically, but true independence and training history require a human audit. A third reviewer adjudicates disagreements without knowing model predictions; record who adjudicated. Report raw agreement; consider kappa and confidence interval after adequate sample size.
4. **Source-, author- and template-disjoint development/test:** groups must not cross splits. Do not split duplicate prompts or rewritten variations randomly. Store test labels in a separate restricted file, not inside GitHub or ordinary development outputs. Source labels may be sensitive; use non-identifying group IDs.
5. **Freeze before test:** full model revision, prompt prototypes, margin rule, any calibration policy, dataset rights, annotation schema, primary/secondary metrics, negative-control inclusion rules, abstention semantics, sample-size rationale and approved test access. Official DDXPlus test is unrelated to this task-intent holdout.
6. **Baseline reporting:** keep M8 original Dense/Hybrid + lexical gate + M9 zero-shot gate as fixed exploratory baselines. Report true symptom retention, symptom false abstention, nonclinical FPR, and subgroup rates (including medical-vocabulary overlap), plus abstention costs. Ranking Recall@3 requires a *separately reviewed disease relevance annotation*. Intent labels alone never establish disease ranking ground truth.

### Minimum CSV schema (all columns required)

`query_id,text,split,source_id,template_family,source_origin,rights_basis,annotator_1_id,annotator_1_label,annotator_2_id,annotator_2_label,final_label,adjudicator_id`

Use `research/intent_cohort_annotation_template.csv` **only as a blank schema**. It intentionally contains no records, approvals or labels.

The `rights_basis` value must be one of `original_synthetic_with_permission`, `licensed_public_with_attribution`, `explicit_consent_documented`. These labels do not verify rights; retain source documents privately. Each annotation must match a supported label. When two reviewers disagree, a third distinct adjudicator is required. Paths under `research/private_data` or `research/outputs` remain gitignored.

## Development-only structural audit

After independent annotations exist at `research/private_data/intent_cohort/development.csv`:

    python -m research.validate_intent_cohort --development research/private_data/intent_cohort/development.csv --out research/outputs/m11-development-audit.json

This audits: distinct reviewers per case; third-party adjudication on disagreements; label counts; raw agreement; rights-basis statements; normalized-text duplicate counts; source/template family counts. It emits **aggregates only**, no raw patient text or query IDs.

The test set remains **blocked** until written external authorization and frozen protocol. `--allow-test` plus a local `--approved-protocol` JSON are required for test-file reads; these are procedural flags, NOT independent verification of permission. Do not create fake approvals to force evaluation. Only after legitimately approved access, structural cross-split overlap checks reject duplicate query IDs, source IDs, template families and normalized text hashes. These checks do not detect all semantic rephrasing leakage.

## M11 success criteria

- Well-documented rights, independently collected author/source groups, complete double annotation, reviewer disagreements resolved.
- A sufficiently diverse prospective development cohort with positive and nonclinical cases, medical-name overlap, out-of-catalog clinical cases, vague symptoms and ambiguous/mixed intents.
- Source/template/near-duplicate audit, frozen model versions and risk–coverage constraints; later a sealed independent test evaluated once.
- Explicit limitations, abstention/failure analyses, and qualified clinical review **before** any patient-facing claims.

**Current blocker:** No new prospective independently annotated cohort has been collected, so M11 implements protocol and software checks only. Do not call it external validation or a conference-ready result.
